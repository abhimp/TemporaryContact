Default retention

Applied to every new contact this server sees. Contacts live at least this long; you can change any contact individually.

Add this on your iPhone

  1. Settings → Contacts → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add CardDAV Account.
  2. Server: your domain. Username & password: your account.
  3. Settings → Contacts → Default Account → choose this account.
  4. In Safari, open this page and “Add to Home Screen” for an app icon.
