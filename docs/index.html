Why

Throwaway contacts, without the mess.

The plumber, the delivery guy, the Airbnb host — contacts you need for a day, not forever. This gives each one an expiry and then forgets it for you.

Auto-expiry

Pick an “expire on” time per contact. It’s deleted automatically once it passes — a floor, never early.

Works everywhere

Standard CardDAV — your contacts show up in Phone, Messages, Spotlight and Contacts on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Google, side by side

Your Google contacts appear live in the same account via a CardDAV proxy. Move a contact to Google to keep it — or back to let it expire.

Nothing lost by accident

Deleted and expired contacts stay recoverable in a Deleted archive for weeks, then quietly purge themselves.

Private & self-hosted

Runs entirely on your own server. Your contacts never touch a third party you don’t control.

A clean control panel

An iOS-style web panel to search, set expiry and manage everything — add it to your home screen as an app.

How it works

Three steps, then forget about it.

1

Deploy

Run it on your server (Python + Radicale) behind nginx or Apache with HTTPS. SQLite or PostgreSQL.

2

Connect

Add the CardDAV account on your phone or Mac and set it as your Default Account for new contacts.

3

Forget cleanup

New contacts expire on their own. Keep the few that matter; the rest disappear when their time is up.

Give every contact an expiry date.

Open source, self-hosted, yours.
